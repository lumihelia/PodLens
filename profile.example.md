# Personal Mapping Profile

Copy this file to `profile.md`, then replace these prompts with any personal
context you want PodLens to use when mapping ideas back to you.

The content is optional and can take any form. Include only context that is
relevant to how you want to understand, compare, or apply source material.
`profile.md` stays local and is excluded from the public site and Git.

## Themes Or Questions I Care About

- Topics, questions, disciplines, or recurring problems I want to notice.

## Current Work

- Projects, responsibilities, research, writing, or decisions that may connect
  to the material.

## Existing Views

- Beliefs, hypotheses, mental models, or open disagreements that new material
  could support, challenge, or refine.

## Relevant Experience Or Constraints

- Background, lived experience, skills, limitations, or context that changes
  what would be useful to me.

## Connections I Want PodLens To Look For

- Concepts, people, prior material, practical applications, or tensions worth
  relating to the source.

## What I Do Not Want

- Types of connections, tone, assumptions, or output that would not be useful.
